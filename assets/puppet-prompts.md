# Painted puppet assets

Created with the built-in imagegen tool. Original transparent PNGs are retained; browser SVG viewport clips isolate each part. Each sheet is 1536 × 1024 with three columns and two rows.

## Jesus — `jesus-puppet.png`

Transparent 3-column by 2-row sprite sheet for a layered animated paper puppet. The same hand-painted theatrical storybook Jesus throughout: warm olive skin, long dark brown hair and beard, ivory robe and dusty blue sash, textured gouache shading, dimensional lighting from upper left. Top row: frontal pleased head; identical head giving a knowing wink; identical head with closed eyes and an exhausted expression. Bottom row: neck-down standing body with robe, sash and sandals, no arms; isolated right arm in ivory sleeve pointing right; isolated relaxed arm with hand down. Equal cells, transparent gutters, no scenery, lettering, grid or halo.

## Foreman — `foreman-puppet.png`

Transparent 3-column by 2-row sprite sheet in the matching painted paper-theatre style. Friendly middle-aged German construction foreman with weathered fair skin, brown moustache, yellow hardhat, navy work overalls, cream shirt, orange safety vest and work boots. Top row: focused frontal head; identical mildly surprised head; identical head looking left with a satisfied smile. Bottom row: neck-down standing body with no arms; isolated reaching arm in a rolled-up cream sleeve; isolated relaxed arm. Equal cells, transparent gutters, no tools, scenery, lettering or grid.

## Corrected left-arm sheets — used by the finished scene

`jesus-left-arm.png` and `foreman-left-arm.png` are imagegen edits of the original sheets. Edit brief: preserve all heads, body and relaxed arm; replace only the bottom-middle part with the anatomically left arm, attached at the viewer-right shoulder, reaching toward the viewer's right. Show natural left-hand anatomy and the back of the hand, with the upper arm sloping toward the elbow. Retain the exact sheet layout, painterly materials, lighting and transparency. Jesus points; the foreman uses a working grasp.

The final renderer uses these corrected sheets, retaining the original sheets here as source references.
